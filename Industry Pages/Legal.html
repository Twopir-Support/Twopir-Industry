Where Firms Stall

Where growth breaks inside a law firm

Firms that scale cleanly control every handoff — from inquiry to signed engagement, from engagement to matter open, from work completed to invoice paid. Every delay compounds.

📥

Intake leaks qualified prospects

Inquiries arrive through forms, calls, referrals and email, then get triaged by hand. Good matters go cold before anyone owns them.

🗂️

Lead management lives outside the core system

When intake sits in spreadsheets and inboxes instead of the CRM, referral attribution is weak, conversion tracking is guesswork, and pipeline visibility always lags.

📁

Case onboarding takes too long

Once a client says yes, staff re-key the same data across intake forms, case management and billing. Opening a matter should take minutes, not days.

💳

Billing and payments stay disconnected

Invoices, retainers, trust balances, accounting entries and matter status live in separate systems — which means slower collections and more reconciliation.

🕐

Operational drag reduces throughput

Manual re-entry, status chasing, duplicated work and missed reminders slow case velocity — almost always because the systems underneath don't talk to each other.

📊

Leadership lacks real-time visibility

Managing partners need clean answers on intake, stalled matters and billing trends. Instead they get spreadsheets assembled after the fact — too late to act on.
