The Real Problem

Fintech Operations Are Becoming More Complex, Not Less.

Financial organizations manage large volumes of customer data, applications, documents, transactions, compliance requirements, and service requests. AI can help fintech companies automate repetitive work while keeping humans involved in important financial and compliance decisions — here's what the data says about where the pressure is coming from.

📋

Loan and onboarding review is still largely manual

Loan and credit applications often require customers to submit extensive information and documents, and teams spend significant time reviewing applications, extracting data, and preparing files for underwriting before a decision is even reached.

🚨

Fraud and AML alert volumes outpace manual review capacity

Fraud and AML teams can receive large numbers of alerts, and the vast majority of financial institutions have already moved to AI-assisted triage because rule-based, manual review can no longer keep pace with transaction volume.

Source: Feedzai 2025 AI Trends Report
💰

Generative AI is projected to reshape banking economics at scale

Generative AI could add the equivalent of 2.8 to 4.7 percent of total industry revenues to global banking annually, largely through increased productivity across sales, service, risk, and operations.

Source: McKinsey Global Institute
🔌

Disconnected CRM, core financial, and compliance systems duplicate work

When CRM, financial, ERP, and document systems don't talk to each other, sales, service, and compliance staff re-key the same customer and transaction information across multiple platforms — a drag on both speed and accuracy.
