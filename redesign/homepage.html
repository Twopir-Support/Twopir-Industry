About Twopir Consulting

The system comes second. The architecture comes first.

Twopir Consulting is a revenue infrastructure consulting partner. We implement, rescue, optimize and integrate the systems that run revenue — even when sales, marketing, service and finance each run their own platform.

We start with how your business actually runs — acquisition, onboarding, service delivery, billing, renewal and expansion — then choose the platforms and automation to support it.

That order matters. It avoids the costliest outcome in enterprise technology — a successful implementation that leaves the same problems in place.

The result is a governed operating model, where acquisition, delivery, billing and renewal become measurable, accountable and continuously optimized.

How We Work

Where Engagements Begin

Organizations come to us at different points — starting a transformation, recovering from one, or running systems that work but no longer scale.

Implement

Build the right foundation from the start. We design and implement Salesforce, HubSpot, AI, integrations and data architecture around your future-state operating model — not around a feature checklist or a copy of the process you already have.

Rescue

Fix what isn't working. Stalled implementations are expensive, and the cause is usually poor architecture, conflicting automation, unreliable data or unclear ownership. We find what is actually creating the problem and redesign it — keeping what works, rebuilding only where necessary.

Optimize

Make the system work better as the business changes. We optimize architecture, automation, data, integrations, security, reporting and governance — and examine the investment itself: which licenses you actually need, and where you're overspending.

Integrate

Connect the systems around your revenue operation. We connect Salesforce and HubSpot with finance, billing, ERP, CPQ, eCommerce and service systems — defining which system owns the data, when it moves, what triggers action, and who owns the process.
